# Roadmap

Done: 12 chapters (161 lessons) for 10 industries, graduation and certificate, live 3D worlds with click-to-inspect,
real three.js support, a start-up-tested bubblewrap sandbox, `input()`, a per-learner workspace and Playground, spaced
review, the mentor view, and packaging (.deb, user install, AppImage script).

## Ideas for next time

1. **Classes and objects, files and modules** as full chapters, and a bigger capstone project, if learners want to go past
   chapter 12.
2. **More industries.** Telecoms, construction, healthcare records, energy. Packs are plain JSON.
3. **Click-to-inspect for everything**: the router, server and PCs in the networking world, trucks' cargo, individual agents.
4. **A second sandbox backend** (firejail) and a sandbox status check on the first run.
5. **Windows and macOS packages.** The app is plain Python and a browser, so a one-click installer is mostly packaging.
6. **A browser test suite in the repository** (Playwright) that completes a lesson, a drill, a review and graduation.
7. **Translations** of the interface and lesson text.
